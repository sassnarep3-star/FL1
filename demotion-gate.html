Demotion gate

Promotion gates answer ‘may this strategy run money?’. Nothing in the repository answered when it stops. These four monitors do that, they run on the real log, and when one fires the consequence is automatic: the strategy goes back to paper, its picks keep being logged, and it cannot come back on profit alone.

C. The four monitors, and why each is written the way it is

MonitorRule as implementedWhy it is defined this waySource
D1 — live decaytrailing mean points beat vs the recorded market number, and its 95% lower bound; a losing run of 60 settled bets also firesnamed D1-SUBSTITUTE: the recorded number is a close or an undated price, so this measures ‘did our edge against the close shrink’, which is strictly weaker than closing-line value. The original D1 needs a timestamped open, which no capture here contains (F49).
D2 — calibration driftslope of realised points beat on stated edge, must stay in [0.4, 1.6]a slope at or below zero is the inversion case: the bigger the claimed edge, the worse the result. This is the monitor that separates ‘unlucky’ from ‘pointing the wrong way’.gen
D3 — concentrationlargest share of net profit from one season (35%) or one club (30%)computed on winning bets only, because that is where concentration actually hides.gen
D4 — data integrity (fail closed)missing market number above 5%, or feed contradictions above 0%added by this session: a strategy cannot be judged on numbers we do not trust. Missing coverage counts as a breach, not as an absence of evidence in the strategy's favour.gen

The thresholds are configuration, published in data/market/demotion_config.json, so a reviewer can change the judgement without touching arithmetic. The definitions on validation.html remain the canonical statement of D1–D3; this page is their evaluation, and the D1 substitution is named rather than hidden.

Merge policy. A D4 trigger demotes strategies and publishes its reason; it does not block a site merge. That reading of the standing rule is written into Q39 so it is a recorded decision rather than an inference from silence.
