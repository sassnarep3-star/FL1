F15 A parallel session's injury CSV had a column-shift bug on multi-injury cells

Cross-check finding Severity Low

A concurrent session independently transcribed the same Week 10 report into data/injuries.csv. Comparing the two transcriptions row by row: all 161 rows it contains also appear in this repo's 345-row capture, with no row present only in theirs. 156 of 161 rows match field for field, which is a useful independent confirmation of the capture. The 5 that differed all involve a player with two listed body parts: 'Ankle, Knee', 'Wrist, Chest', 'Knee, Achilles', 'Ankle, Triceps' and 'Ankle, Knee'. Because that CSV was comma-delimited and the injury field was unquoted, the second body part shifted into the adjacent columns, so the practice and game status fields were displaced. This repo's capture preserves the full published value and the correct game status, and the raw page capture confirms it (e.g. A.J. Brown 'Ankle, Knee / Limited Participation / Questionable').

Sources consulted: S01

What was done instead. This repo keeps its own capture, which was verified line by line against the raw page. The other session's files are preserved in archive/v2/ for reference. Any future CSV export must quote every field containing a comma.

F25 Computed-layer leaderboard's breakeven_hit is circular: 1/(1+realized ROI), not the price hurdle

Data methodology Severity High

scripts/strategy_lab.py computes avg_dec as the mean of (realized profit/stake + 1) over settled bets - losers contribute 0 - so breakeven_hit = 1/(1+realized ROI) and gate G4 ('beats_vig') is tested against a quantity derived from the outcomes being tested. Every spread strategy at ordinary prices is then failed by construction (e.g. WIND15_UNDER: breakeven_hit 0.9326 = 1/1.0724 = 1/(1+ROI 0.0723)). The per-bet odds ARE present in that pipeline's bet ledger (home_spread_odds etc.), so the correct price-based hurdle is computable there with a one-line fix. Nothing in the computed layer was edited by this session; the value is transcribed as dataset_breakeven_hit_transcribed in data/leaderboard_signal_staking.json and NOT used for ranking - the signal split ranks against the standard -110 reference hurdle instead and labels it.

Sources consulted: S47

What was done instead. SG24: recompute breakeven from per-bet odds in the computed layer and re-run its gates; until then treat its G4 column as unusable.
