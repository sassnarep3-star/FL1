Price Snapshots

The store records opening and closing numbers for every market we originate. The canonical store is engine/price_snapshots.py: append-only, source-bound (every quote must carry a source URL, a source record id, a capture timestamp and the model version that produced the market key), idempotent on duplicate delivery, and fail-closed on anything it cannot attribute. This page is its export for the Week 10 slate.

Opening prices are blank on purpose. No verified source of NFL opening lines exists in this repository — the third-party snapshot holds closes only. An opener inferred from a close would be a fabrication, so the columns exist and stay empty until a priced feed is acquired (open question Q21, flags F18 and F23). The store is ready: record() takes phase="open" today.

How to read the phase column

close means the game is played and the third-party feed records the closing line — though the feed publishes no capture timestamp for the close, which is why that cell is blank. The one already-played game of the slate (WAS at NYG) is in this state. intermediate means the game is not played: the value is the latest observed line as of the 2026-11-14 snapshot and is not a close. Closing values for those games will replace these rows as additional capture phases, not edits.
