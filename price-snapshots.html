Price-Snapshot Store

Requirement R21: the store records the opening and the closing number for every market we originate — one row per (game, market, quote side), created before kickoff and completed at close. A price cell is filled only when a named source record backs it. Rows are stored in the mandated origination order: SIDE, then TOTAL, then MONEYLINE (R23).

Opening prices: 0 of 56 rows have one. No source of opening prices exists in this repository — not official, not third-party — so every open cell is blank and recorded as blank. That is the store doing its job: the gap is now a countable, fillable field rather than a silence. Question Q21 and suggestions SG20/SG25 own the path forward.
