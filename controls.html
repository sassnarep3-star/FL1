Control baselines

Every leaderboard run now carries three yardsticks that need no insight: random picks (fixed seed 20261119), favourites only, and the original bet every home team control that measures the house margin. A strategy has to beat both the random and the favourites controls on the same proper score before it can be called skill. Right now that comparison cannot be made for any candidate.

Status: the two new controls are computed, scored and published in data/controls.csv. Candidates scored: 0 of 15. Reason: candidate strategies store a bet and its result, not the probability they assigned to it (requirement R35). Until that exists, the statement “beats both controls” is NOT EVALUABLE, not passed.

Data: these controls use the third-party nflverse games feed (S47), which is not an official NFL source. They are flagged, and the values are not presented as official. See Flagged for Review.

3. How the controls are defined

  • Random: one side per game, drawn with Python’s random.Random(20261119) (stable across Python releases). Forecast p = 0.5 for the side it picked, so its Brier score is 0.25 by construction. Its hit rate and ROI are the realised check.
  • Favourites only: always the side the market favours. Forecast p = that favourite’s cover rate on the training seasons 2006-2019 only (fixed before the out-of-sample games are scored, so the control never sees its own test data).
  • Sign convention (verified, not assumed): in this feed, spread_line > 0 means the home side is the favourite. The home team is the moneyline favourite in 99.4% of 5,198 regular-season games with both prices, and the home margin correlates +0.43 with the positive spread. The repository’s line-board label “negative = home favourite” contradicts this and is logged as an irregularity (F73).
  • Brier interval: mean ± 1.96 standard errors, one bet per game, so the game is the unit. Pushes are excluded, as on every other row.
