Signal, Staking & Demotion

How the leaderboard keeps a lucky staking run from hiding a bad signal, when a live strategy goes back to paper, and why no correlation value is published yet.

1. Signal versus staking

The board ranks on the signal alone: the 95% lower bound of the equal-weight per-unit return of every settled bet. Staking is reported beside it as dollar-weighted yield and cannot change the rank. A staking reward needs both the signal and the staking bound to be above zero, so staking can add to a strong signal but cannot rescue a weak one.

Signal is the equal-weight per-unit return (ranks the board). Staking is the dollar-weighted yield (reported, never ranks). Values are produced by engine/leaderboard.py.

2. Demotion gate G-14: live back to paper

A live strategy returns to paper when a trigger fires. Paper is not a penalty box: the strategy keeps accumulating paper bets, and only a full window of positive paper results returns it to live. Thresholds are proposals for owner decision (see the suggestions register).

3. Correlations: official sources only

Brief B6: correlations are computed only from officially sourced numbers, and n is published with each value. The 2026 official results and the official injury report do not contain enough joint observations for a correlation, and every priced pair needs official prices that do not exist. Nothing is published as a coefficient.

Correlation values are withheld under brief B6. The sample size n is kept for every pair. The source of n is a third-party mirror (nflverse), so it is shown as a sample size only, not a verified fact.
