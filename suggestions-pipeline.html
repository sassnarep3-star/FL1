FL1 — NFL Line Lab — Verified data, open questions, and a strategy leaderboard that refuses to guess.

Data captured 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-16 (UTC) from official league and club sources. Every value links to its source. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. Nothing on this site is a bet, an offer to wager, or advice. No price, edge or expected-value figure is displayed unless an official source supports it.
