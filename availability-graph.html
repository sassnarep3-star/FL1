The availability graph

Who was expected to start, and who actually did. This page joins two official documents that had never been joined before: the clubs' own published depth charts, and the NFL's own Game Book for the same game. On the left is what the club said its order would be; on the right is what the league recorded. Where the two disagree, the disagreement is the signal — and the depth of the replacement is measured on the club's own chart, not invented.

Where this stops, and why

The club depth charts in this repository were captured for Week 10 of 2026, so they are only a valid statement of expectation for Week 10 games. Using them to explain an earlier game would be lookahead, and the graph does not do that.

Some clubs print the word “unofficial” on their own depth chart. That is recorded against the row, and the official game book — not the chart — is the authority on who played.

A player the chart names at the first string who does not appear in the game book at all is reported as not on the game-day roster. The book does not say whether that means injured reserve, a trade or a release, so this page does not guess.
