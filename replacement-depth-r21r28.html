Replacement Depth

Requirement R26: track the depth of the replacement, not just the fact of a replacement. A club missing its starter and its backup is not the same problem as a club missing only its starter. This page names, for every affected slot, which string the next available player is.

What is observed, and what is not. The chart order and string numbers are published by the clubs themselves (labelled unofficial — sources per row). The game status is the official injury report (S01). A string counts as missing only on an official Out; Doubtful and Questionable are notes, never absences. Nothing here claims who will actually take the field: no snap-count source is verified (question Q27).
