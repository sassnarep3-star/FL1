Correlation gate

Two rules, enforced by a function rather than by a promise: a correlation may be published only if every operand of it comes from an official source, and every published value carries the number of joint observations it rests on. A number derived from a third-party price feed is not false — it is a measurement of that dataset — but a column headed ‘correlation’ invites a reader to take it for a fact about the league, so those rows are computed, labelled and held.

Held rows are computed and shown, including the ρ the gate refuses to publish, so the reader can see the shape of the missing evidence. The pair the parlay pricing most wants — the over against the home cover, measured on market numbers — cannot be published on these rules because the league publishes no prices (F17).
