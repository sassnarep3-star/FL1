Executive Summary

High-level overview of the FL1 NFL decision-support and strategy project.
← Back to Dashboard

Purpose

FL1 collects, organizes, and analyzes NFL information to improve decision making. The system originates its own NFL lines, generates betting signals, tracks injuries and team deficiencies (starters vs. backups on offense and defense), and provides a reviewable suggestions process so changes are chosen deliberately before implementation.

Key Requirements

Suggestions & Review Section

A dedicated section where suggestions can be reviewed and the best path chosen before any change is implemented. Includes open questions, required information, candidate answers, and missing-information discovery steps.

NFL Line Origination & Strategies

Generate useful signals for single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos. Every strategy passes validation gates; correlation values are determined; luck is removed in favor of selectively skill-based strategies tracked on a rewarded leaderboard.

Injury & Deficiency Tracking

Track injuries and identify defensive/offensive deficiencies, including 2nd, 3rd, and 4th string backups replacing starters due to lineup changes.

No Manual Input

No manual data entry by the user. Work proceeds autonomously, verified line by line against official trusted sources.

Verified Data Only

Every table row carries a source link to an official site. Unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged unverified; nothing is inferred or filled in.

Live GitHub Pages Site

Clean landing page plus category subpages, dashboard UI, entries/findings in easy-to-read tables, analysis, findings, and a labeled Flagged for Review page.
