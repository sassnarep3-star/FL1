What the analyses do — and do not — establish

The official-results panel computes a home-win rate over the frozen sample. It does not establish that a model has an edge. Separately, the 16-entry signal panel reports no strategy clearing its family-wise threshold. That panel is based on a third-party-backed research ledger, not an NFL publication; its result should not be combined with the official-results panel.

F66 records overlapping readiness and luck implementations; SG47 leaves consolidation for owner review. The Dashboard and topic navigation keep the competing views available with their distinct inputs and definitions. No view is declared canonical here. See F66, SG47, S47, the family-wise signal panel, and the official-results luck panel.

Some market and backtest outputs use third-party price data. They are labelled as third-party and remain subject to the unresolved source-policy question. They are not official NFL facts, and no result in this summary is a recommendation to wager.
