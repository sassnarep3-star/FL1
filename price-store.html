Price-snapshot store

Every number this project quotes as “the market line” is a row in an append-only ledger, with the feed it came from, the field of that feed, the moment the capture was taken, and what the publisher actually asserted about when in the game-week the price was struck. The brief asked for opening and closing numbers for every market we originate. This is that store, and the coverage table below is the honest size of the first half of that sentence.

The opening half of requirement 1, stated plainly. A first-posted price exists in these captures for exactly one season — 2021, one sportsbook, no odds column, and a file whose last upstream change predates the season it covers. It is stored, hashed, quarantined from pricing and settlement, and published in full. Closing numbers exist for 3,415 games across the seasons the feeds cover. Nothing was labelled an opener to make the table look complete. Flag F43 and Q35 carry the consequence.

Where the artefacts are

Snapshot ledger exports, coverage, drift, quarantine and the capture manifest live in data/market/ (see S76) and are rebuilt byte-identically by python3 scripts/build_market_spine.py --check. The SQLite store itself is the production write path: engine/price_snapshots.py.
