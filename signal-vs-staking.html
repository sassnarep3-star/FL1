Signal vs staking

The leaderboard separates signal (what the picks are worth at flat stakes) from staking (what a betting policy did to the bankroll), so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal. Only the signal ledger ranks, rewards and promotes.

The rule (gate G-17, hard). Rank order, reward eligibility and capital promotion read the SIGNAL columns exclusively. The staking ledger is published beside it as risk telemetry: it can cap or remove exposure — and a staking risk breach triggers the demotion gate — but it can never promote. In the current candidate set, 3 entrants show exactly that failure mode: positive staking profit over a non-positive signal — DOME_OVER, __CONTROL_MARKET, DOME_UNDER. They carry the MASKED flag below.

Demotion gate G-16 — a live strategy goes back to paper

Promotion is slow (two reviews); demotion is fast (one trigger) and automatic. Gate G-17 above is the ranking contract it enforces.

FieldRuleSource
GateDemotion gate: live -> paper (HARD — automatic)gen
Trigger A — signal decayrolling 250-bet closing-line-value lower bound < 0 at two consecutive monthly evaluations, OR signal ROI below break-even over the trailing 150 settled bets with the one-sided test p > 0.5 (indistinguishable from losing)gen
Trigger B — risk breachdrawdown from live peak exceeds 50% of allocated bankroll at live stakes, OR stake cap exceeded, OR a ledger audit invalidates settled results (source correction)gen
Trigger C — complianceany G-12 failure (prohibited market class, unregistered input, non-public information) — immediate, no evaluation windowgen
DestinationPAPER (Stage 2 exposure): zero capital, sealed evaluation continues, the entry keeps its record and its graveyard rightsgen
Executionautomatic at the evaluation checkpoint by the system; no discretion, no appeal; the demotion event is written to the strategy's sealed loggen
Re-entryback through the normal ladder from paper: a fresh sealed window of >= 150 settled paper bets with positive CLV lower bound AND all hard gates re-cleared on that window; a demoted strategy may not re-enter live on the strength of its old recordgen
Asymmetrystaking outcomes can NEVER promote (promotion reads signal columns only), but a staking risk breach CAN demote. A lucky staking run masks nothing; a dangerous one is punished.gen
WhyThe failure mode of every strategy programme is the edge that died quietly while the bankroll looked fine. G-11 monitors decay; G-16 is the enforcement: the strategy stops carrying capital the moment the evidence stops supporting it.gen

Luck controls this design stands on

  • Break-even arithmetic: −110 needs 52.38%; separating a true 55% from that at 80% power needs 2,243 settled bets — see Validation Gates.
  • Ranking on lower confidence bounds and deflated significance, not profit (measured layer).
  • Sealed entries, overlap merging and abstention logging (anti-gaming rules in the leaderboard design).
