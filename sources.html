Sources

Every fact on this site resolves to one of these records. A source marked VERIFIED was loaded in this session and its content read. A source marked NOT VERIFIED failed to load, and nothing on this site is asserted on the strength of it.

Why some sources fail. Three official NFL addresses that are widely cited returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-14, including the injury-report policy PDF. Rather than quote a document we could not open, the policy text is omitted and the gap is listed on Flagged for Review.

Tiers. Official (league) means published by the NFL. Official (club) means published by a club, which speaks only for that club. Official (government) means a US federal agency.
