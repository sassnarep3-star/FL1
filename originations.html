Line origination

We originate our own numbers. The order is mandated and fixed: the side first, then the total, then the moneyline. Every market we originate publishes three separate columns — the model’s fair line, the market line, and the difference — and no cell is ever inferred.

Origination order

  • Stage 1 — Side (game spread). The margin is the anchor quantity. Total and moneyline both decompose from the same scoring view; originating the side first fixes the model's committed margin before anything dependent is priced. (ORIGINATED)
  • Stage 2 — Total (game points). The total is the second quantity the model must commit to (pace/efficiency view). It is published as a column set so the gap is visible, but the fair value stays BLANK because no parameter has been fitted — filling it would be a fabrication. (BLOCKED — structure published, fair line blank)
  • Stage 3 — Moneyline (game winner). The moneyline is a restatement of the side's win probability in price form; originating it last prevents it from leaking information back into the margin. (ORIGINATED)

A game is re-originated in this order at every release. The side is the anchor quantity: total and moneyline are downstream of the same scoring decomposition, and committing the margin first stops dependent markets from leaking information back into it.

The three-column rule

  • Fair line — MODEL OUTPUT. Causal pre-game Elo, versioned: fl1-elo-v1 (K=20, HFA=48, inter-season regress x0.75 toward 1505, MOV multiplier ln(mov+1)*2.2/(|elo_gap_hfa|*0.001+2.2), spread=10.8*log10(p/(1-p)), p clipped [0.02,0.98]).
  • Market line — SOURCE. The pinned third-party snapshot data/raw/games.csv (nflverse/nfldata, S47), flagged — no official NFL price source exists (flag F34).
  • Difference — COMPUTED. Fair minus market. Spread columns use the away-posted unit of the snapshot (positive = away underdog / home favoured): negative difference = the market gives the away side more points than the model says is fair — value on the away side at the market number; positive = value on the home side. Moneyline differences are published in implied-probability percentage points against the market’s raw implied probability (vig included).
Worked example (2026 W1, NE @ SEA). The market number is SEA’s side “NE +3” (away-posted +3). The model’s fair away number is +0.9 — it makes Seattle a smaller favourite than the market does, so the difference is −2.1 and the value read is on the away side (NE +3). The game finished SEA 13–10: NE +3 pushed, which is exactly what a ±0-model-edge on a 3-point line looks like.

Origination policy

  • Every release re-originates in this order: side, total, moneyline.
  • A model number is never published without its model version and basis (pre-game causal vs snapshot).
  • A market number is never published without its provenance label (third-party flagged until a licensed/official price feed lands behind the price-snapshot store).
  • The difference column is computed, never typed: fair minus market, with the sign convention stated on the page.
  • Player props and parlays are NOT originated in v1 (prop settlement definitions and joint pricing are blocked — see questions.html).
