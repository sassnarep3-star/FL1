Signal, and staking, kept apart

One leaderboard, two blocks. The signal block never sees a stake: it measures points beat the line, hit rate against the break-even implied by the log's own prices, whether the stated edge is calibrated, and how much of the available slate the rule selected. The staking block measures what a bankroll experienced. A run of luck can move the second block; it cannot move the first, and only the first ranks.
