Requirement R22: backtests and ROI can be computed at all only if there is a results feed whose every row is checked against an official source. This is that feed: 137 finished games, weeks 1–10 of the 2026 season, every row official (S52), every row settled against by engine/backtest.py and by nothing else.

Verification, not transcription. Recomputing every club's W/L/T and PF/PA from this feed reproduces the official standings capture exactly — 32 of 32 clubs — as part of the build (scripts/build_results_feed.py fails loudly otherwise). The raw capture is raw/schedule-results-2026-week1-10.tsv; tools/verify_results.py re-checks it.
