Verified results feed

Every played game of the 2026 regular season with its final score, verified row by row against official NFL.com week pages — the settlement source of truth backtests and ROI are computed against. 137 games, weeks 1–10.

Verification (2026-11-14). Each of the 137 finals was transcribed from the official week page’s own “FINAL” line into raw/official-finals-2026.tsv, then compared field for field against two independent captures (data/results.tsv, data/schedule2026-d.csv). scripts/verify_feed_m.py reports 137/137 PASS, zero mismatches. The same files were previously reconciled to the official standings (records + PF + PA, 32/32 clubs) — see Verification and Schedule 2026.
Parallel implementations preserved (F31). The same eight requirements were built three times concurrently. This page is the merged superset. The R21-R28 sprint's own feed page stays at verified-results-feed.html, and its line/price work at Line Board and Price Snapshots. Cross-checks between the independent captures are a feature, not a conflict.

What a backtest can and cannot show

  • Calibration and error: yes — walk-forward, each week predicted only from earlier weeks, n on every row (below).
  • ROI against official prices: no — no official league source prices betting lines (F18, F34). ROI columns against third-party snapshots are published on the strategy pages, labelled third-party throughout.
  • Own-line honesty (F20): the own-line fair model does not beat a constant benchmark out of sample on side or total MAE. Published because negative results are part of the record.

Why a feed, and what it makes computable

  • Settlement. Side, total and moneyline bets settle against these scores only. engine/results_settlement.py refuses to settle or compute ROI on a price whose provenance is not declared — market prices are NOT in the feed; they arrive from the price-snapshot store or a declared third-party snapshot with a source id.
  • ROI and backtests. roi() computes stake-normalised profit with pushes removed from both numerator and denominator, and fails closed before producing a single number if any bet lacks price provenance. 17 unit tests (tests/test_results_feed.py).
  • What the feed does not contain. No prices, no player statistics, no snap counts. Blanks stay blank; nothing is inferred.
