Validation Gates

Eight gates. A strategy must pass every one that applies to it before it can be ranked. The gates are defined here, before any strategy exists, precisely so they cannot be rewritten to fit a result.

Removing luck: three separate tests, never one blended score

The brief asks to remove luck from the equation. That is only meaningful if it is operational, so it is defined as three tests that must each hold. They are reported side by side and are never averaged, because averaging lets a strong result on one hide a failure on another.

Correlation

A parlay's price depends on the joint probability of its legs. If two legs are positively correlated, multiplying their individual probabilities understates the joint probability and misstates the edge. The reverse is true for negatively correlated legs.

Correlation is therefore estimated from repeated joint outcomes of the same pairing, and never assumed. A pairing needs many observations before an estimate means anything; below that threshold the value stays blank.

Acceptance thresholds

The numeric thresholds are deliberately not set on this page. Setting them after seeing a result is the single easiest way to manufacture apparent skill, and choosing them before a benchmark price exists would be arbitrary. They are registered as open question Q09 and must be fixed in one commit before any grading begins.

Demotion gate — live to paper

A strategy that has been promoted to live is not entitled to stay there. The demotion gate is the honest counterpart to the promotion gates: it defines when a live strategy is sent back to paper (research) so a decaying edge never keeps running capital.

GateNameTestAction on failPrinciple
D1Live-decay monitorRolling CLV lower bound stays below zero across the monitoring window.Auto-demote to research; no discretionary rescue.An edge that existed and died should not run capital.gen
D2Calibration driftRealised hit rate falls outside the stated probability bucket band on the holdout.Demote and re-fit; re-clear G1-G6 before returning to live.Stated probabilities must stay well calibrated.gen
D3Concentration breachOne season, team, opponent cluster or stadium exceeds its allowed share of net PnL.Demote and reduce exposure; re-clear G8 before returning to live.One lucky year against three bad teams is not an edge.gen

The demotion thresholds are fixed before the first live stake and reviewed on the same schedule as the promotion gates, never after a losing streak. Because D1 needs a benchmark price to measure CLV and no official price source exists in this repository (flag F08), D1-D3 are defined here but are not yet active. No strategy can be promoted to live until a price source is supplied and the demotion rule is registered.

G-14 — the demotion gate (requirement R28): live strategies return to paper

Parallel build (F31). The D1–D3 demotion gate above (from Signal, Staking & Demotion) and this G-14 gate are two independent implementations of requirement R28 with different triggers and thresholds. Both are preserved; Q24 decides which becomes canonical.

A gate set decides who gets promoted. This gate decides who gets sent back. Without it, a live badge outlives the edge that earned it and the leaderboard decays into a graveyard of former winners. Any ONE trigger demotes; the verdict logic is not reviewable, the thresholds are.

States: LIVE → (any trigger) → PAPER; PAPER → LIVE only through re-promotion on a fresh sealed sample. Old bets never re-qualify an entry — the burden of proof resets. Luck panel — where the three tests meet their numbers (G-19 panel, session 2026-11-16)

The removal-of-luck tests above now have a published numeric panel on the dashboard and the full table on Decision Readiness: per-strategy p(luck alone this hot), the family-wise bar (α/16 = 0.00313), the sample floor to certify an observed rate at 80 percent power, and the smallest edge each sample could certify. Verdict: NO STRATEGY CLEARING — the best raw p-value (0.1236, DIV_DOG_AND_ELO) is far from the bar, so nothing is promoted and the reward pool pays 0 units.

G-18 — drift alarm on every scraper

The brief: pin each parser to expected row counts and required fields; fail the build loudly when a field goes missing rather than publishing a blank. tools/verify_drift.py pins 19 datasets (row counts + required columns) and rewrites data/verification-drift.tsv on every run:

  • FAIL (exit 1): a dataset shrinks, a required column disappears, or a required column goes fully empty — the build refuses to publish.
  • WARN (exit 0): a dataset grows — legitimate (a new week settles), but the pin must be re-committed with the growth, in the open.

Pins cover: injuries 345, standings 32, results 137, schedule 272, results feed 137, price snapshots 56, settled markets 548, questions 34, suggestions 34, flags 42, strategies 6 (incl. the change-of-mind fields), gates 15, signal-staking 16, sources (columns), reserve list 25, announced starters 4, venue spot-checks 2, luck panel 16, ablations 2. Run: python3 tools/verify_drift.py — wired into CI beside the other gates.
