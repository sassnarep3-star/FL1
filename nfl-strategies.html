1. Line Origination Approach

The system originates its own NFL lines by comparing an internal win-probability model against the market line. Edge = internal probability − implied market probability. A bet is only signalled when edge exceeds a validation-gate threshold.

4. Correlation Determination

Correlation between legs is measured so correlated parlay/prop legs are not treated as independent. Proposed method: Pearson/Spearman correlation on historical outcomes plus conditional probability P(B|A) and variance-inflation checks. Exact method and cap are a reviewer decision (Suggestions Q-04).

Rewards are allocated to top-ranked strategies only after each strategy passes all validation gates and performance is verified against a third-party odds source (not an official NFL source).

Removing Luck — Skill-Based Filter

To remove luck, a strategy is retained only if: (1) it passes every validation gate, (2) its edge holds out-of-sample, (3) correlation is accounted for, and (4) performance remains above threshold across multiple seasons. Strategies failing any gate are excluded from the leaderboard.
