Line Board — fair | market | difference

Parallel build (F31). A second implementation of this requirement lives at Model Lines & Price Store, which keeps every market and difference cell blank under a strict source policy. This page publishes the pinned capture with a THIRD_PARTY badge. Both are preserved; Q23 decides which becomes canonical.

Requirement R24: the model's fair line, the market line and the difference are published as three separate columns, in the mandated origination order (R23): side first, then total, then moneyline. difference = model − market. A blank difference is a blank, never a zero.

Conventions, fixed before results. Side quotes are HOME-view spreads (negative = home favourite). The pinned market feed records the AWAY club's spread — verified against moneyline direction across 13/13 games (check V8) — so the market column negates the verbatim capture and says so per row; the verbatim number sits in the price-snapshot store. The moneyline market cell is blank because no moneyline price was captured (F25).

How to read the difference column

A positive difference means the model's number is higher than the market's in the displayed convention. The column measures disagreement, not edge: a difference is only actionable once the band excludes the market number AND the prices in the snapshot store clear their hurdle. The market column carries the THIRD_PARTY badge because no official league source prices NFL lines (F23, question Q23 — the strict-blank policy is one review away).
