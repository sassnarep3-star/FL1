Origination Board

Every market we originate appears here with the model's fair line, the market line and the difference as three separate columns — never a blended number. Markets are originated side first, then total, then moneyline, and the order is enforced by engine/origination.py, not by habit.

The fair column is blank (F24). A fitted fair-line model exists on Model Lines but has not passed acceptance: its own flag records that it does not beat a constant benchmark out of sample (F20). A number in this column today would present an unaccepted model as accepted. The difference column is blank with it, because a difference computed from one arm is not a difference. The market column is blank too: third-party snapshot values are recorded in the audit layer (data/line_board.csv) but not displayed without an official price source (F18/F25).
