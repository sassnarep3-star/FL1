Origination Board

Every market we originate appears here with the model's fair line, the market line and the difference as three separate columns — never a blended number. Markets are originated side first, then total, then moneyline, and the order is enforced by engine/origination.py, not by habit.

The fair column is blank (F19). The model specification leaves every coefficient deliberately unfitted; a number in that column today would be fabricated. The difference column is blank with it, because a difference computed from one arm is not a difference. The market column carries third-party snapshot values with their source (F20) — no official NFL price source exists.
