Depth of the replacement

An absence is not a quantity. ‘Starting weakside linebacker is out’ tells a reader nothing about how much worse the team is; what matters is who fills in and how far down that club's own published chart the replacement sits. So every vacancy here is resolved to a named person, and the replacement is scored on the chart.

What this page refuses to do. It publishes an ordinal depth measurement, not a points penalty. Converting ‘two strings deep’ into ‘minus 2.1 points’ needs snap counts and player-quality estimates no official source publishes, so the conversion is left named and blank in the model register instead of invented here. The chart is also unofficial by name — clubs publish it as a listing, not as a rules-defined depth order (F02).
