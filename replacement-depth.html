Replacement Depth

The brief is explicit: track the depth of the replacement, not just the fact of a replacement. For every charted player carrying an official game status, this table names the next player the club itself lists at that slot and records which string he is. A fourth-string replacement is a different event from a second-string one, and a flat “someone is out” hides the difference.

What this table does not claim

No snap counts were verified, so the table never claims the listed replacement actually played. It states who the club lists next, at what string, and what the official report says about him — three observed facts. A player designated Doubtful or Questionable as the next man is recorded as the replacement with his own status visible, never skipped and never upgraded.
