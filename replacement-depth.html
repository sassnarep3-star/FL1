Replacement depth

Not just the fact of a replacement — the depth of it. When a starter carries an official game status, this page names the player the club’s own depth chart lists next, his string (2nd, 3rd, 4th), and how many listed players are Out above him. Week 10, 2026: 69 affected lineup spots — 34 on offense, 35 on defense.

Rule (derived, published with every row). Inputs are the official Week 10 injury report (S01) and the clubs’ own published depth charts (per-club sources, chips in the table). Name joins use only the explicit alias table — never fuzzy matching. Players whose official game status is “Out” are skipped; the first listed player remaining is the replacement, and his published rank at that lineup spot is his string. A player absent from the club’s report is labelled “not listed” — an official fact, and per league policy absence is not evidence of availability.

Headline findings

  • 1 replacement(s) come from 3rd string or deeper — the brief’s “2nd/3rd/4th string backup” case, carried with its cascade count.
  • 6 spots have no listed replacement remaining (every player the club lists there is Out): those cells are BLANK and flagged, never filled in.
  • Coverage is 31 of 32 clubs — Houston publishes its chart as an image (flag F16), so no Houston rows exist rather than invented ones.
  • League-wide historical depth-of-replacement tracking (starters-not-primary, max sequence rank, 1999–2026) is published on Backup Depth and in the computed availability model.
