Point-in-time snapshot ledger

An injury report is not a weekly fact, it is a series that changes several times between Wednesday and kickoff. This page keeps every dated read of the official report, each with its own hash, and publishes the diff between consecutive reads instead of overwriting the old one.

Between 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-16 the official page gained 2 rows and moved 32 fields. Most of the movement is the Chargers, who played on Monday night and therefore received game-day designations after the earlier read was taken.
