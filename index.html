How decisions are made here

  1. All entries are recorded in tables with an official source link.
  2. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged unverified.
  3. Suggestions are collected and reviewed before any change is implemented — no change is made on manual input alone.
  4. Betting strategies must pass validation gates and demonstrate skill-based (non-luck) performance on a leaderboard.
  5. Irregularities are published on the Flagged for Review page for your review.
