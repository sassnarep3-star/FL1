What this site is

A verified, auditable base for originating NFL lines. It separates three things that are usually blurred together: what an official source actually says, what we do not know yet, and what we propose to do about it. Nothing here has been estimated to fill a gap. Where a number could not be confirmed, the cell is blank.

No strategy on this site has a performance figure, because no strategy has been run. A blank leaderboard is the honest starting state.

Removal of luck — why the board is still empty

A strategy's luck-removed edge is the 95% LOWER bound of its hit rate minus the break-even hurdle at the price paid (0.52381 at standard -110). A lucky run raises ROI but it also narrows nothing: the lower bound only rises with more settled evidence, so it cannot be gamed by variance.

The one-line proof. The register's top ROI entry (+5.73%, +53.7 units) is REJECTED because its luck-removed edge is negative (-1.31pp): 936 settled bets give a 95% hit-rate lower bound of 51.07% against a 52.38% break-even hurdle.

Control baseline. Betting every home team at the close loses 5.19% over 5,067 settled bets — that loss is the house margin. Every strategy is judged against this yardstick, not against zero.

Multiplicity. Gate G-6 deflates for the number of trials; the register transcribes G6_adjusted_alpha=0.00333 per row. With 16 strategies tried, a p-value that looks fine alone is not fine for the family.

Dataset tier. The underlying dataset is the computed research layer over third-party data (flags F19/F23); its breakeven_hit column is circular (F30) and is NOT used here. The OFFICIAL register has no settled bet log yet, so no entry can be ranked. The panel demonstrates the method on the flagged dataset — it does not endorse any strategy.
