Signal-versus-variance view — 2026-11-16

This is the separately retained luck-removed-edge view in data/luck_panel_signal.json (updated 2026-11-16), calculated on the third-party-backed computed research layer. It uses the 95% hit-rate lower bound minus the standard -110 break-even; ROI is telemetry, not a ranking criterion. It is distinct from the family-wise p-value view below, and from the official-results-only 137-game panel on Removal of Luck. A strategy's luck-removed edge is the 95% LOWER bound of its hit rate minus the break-even hurdle at the price paid (0.52381 at standard -110). A lucky run raises ROI but it also narrows nothing: the lower bound only rises with more settled evidence, so it cannot be gamed by variance.

The one-line proof. The register's top ROI entry (+5.73%, +53.7 units) is REJECTED because its luck-removed edge is negative (-1.31pp): 936 settled bets give a 95% hit-rate lower bound of 51.07% against a 52.38% break-even hurdle.

Control baseline. Betting every home team at the close loses 5.19% over 5,067 settled bets — that loss is the house margin. Every strategy is judged against this yardstick, not against zero.

Multiplicity. Gate G-6 deflates for the number of trials; the register transcribes G6_adjusted_alpha=0.00333 per row. With 16 strategies tried, a p-value that looks fine alone is not fine for the family.

Dataset tier. The underlying dataset is the computed research layer over third-party data (flags F19/F23); its breakeven_hit column is circular (F30) and is NOT used here. The OFFICIAL register has no settled bet log yet, so no entry can be ranked. The panel demonstrates the method on the flagged dataset — it does not endorse any strategy.

It differs from the signal-versus-variance hit-rate lower-bound panel above and from the official-results-only 137-game panel on Removal of Luck.

16 strategies evaluated · 0 proven · verdict: NO STRATEGY CLEARING. The family-wise bar for a 16-strategy search is a p-value below 0.00313 (Bonferroni α/16); the best raw p-value is 0.1236 (DIV_DOG_AND_ELO), so no strategy has separated itself from luck. The panel publishes the probability that luck ALONE would run at least this hot — a p-value, never the probability that a strategy has no edge.
