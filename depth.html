Backup Depth & Deficiencies

The brief asks which 2nd, 3rd and 4th string players are being forced in. That question has two halves, and only one of them can be answered from official sources today. This page keeps them apart.

Half 1 — Observed absence OFFICIAL

Who is officially unavailable, and at which depth slot they are listed. Read straight from the injury report and the club depth chart. No inference.

Half 2 — Replacement measured NOT AVAILABLE

Whether a backup actually took the snaps requires snap counts. No official snap-count source was verified, so this half is left blank rather than asserted. See F09.

Why only one club. nfl.com/depth-charts/ returns 404, so there is no league-wide official depth chart. Depth has to come club by club, and the one club chart captured in full is titled “Unofficial Depth Chart” by the club itself. One club is therefore worked end to end as a verified demonstration rather than 32 half-checked tables. See F02, F10 and suggestion SG13.

What this page will not do

  • It will not call a backup “the starter this week” without a snap-count source.
  • It will not rank depth beyond the club's own published ordering, and that ordering is labelled unofficial.
  • It will not treat an absent player as injured. Absent and injured are different facts.
