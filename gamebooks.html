Official game books

The official record of what actually happened on the field. Every row on this page is read from an NFL Game Book — the document the league publishes after every game — and every row links to the game it came from. This is the evidence the injury report cannot give: who was inactive, not merely who was on the report, and who started, not merely who was listed first on a depth chart.

What a game book does not contain

The NFL Game Book publishes the starting lineup, the substitutions, the “Did Not Play” list, the “Not Active” list, the venue, the roof and surface status, and the game-day weather. It does not publish per-player snap counts for offensive players, so this repository does not claim to have snap counts. Depth that is measured from playing time remains blocked on an official snap-count source; see the requirement backlog.
