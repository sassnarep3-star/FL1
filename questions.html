Open Questions

20 questions, 10 blocking. A question is marked blocking when no defensible number can be produced until it is answered. The blocking ones are the reason the leaderboard is empty.

Q09What sample size would make a strategy's result distinguishable from luck, and how long is one NFL season?CriticalYes — for the leaderboardA 2026 regular season runs 18 weeks; Week 10 is in progress and the league calendar shows Week 18 on Jan 9-10, 2027, with Super Bowl LXI on Feb 14, 2027 (S05). Q10How is 'luck removed' defined operationally, rather than as a slogan?HighNoThree measurable proxies exist with data we can actually get: (a) calibration — do 60% predictions win 60% of the time; (b) closing-line value — did the fair value move toward our number; (c) persistence — does the edge hold out of sample and across seasons.Which proxy the user will accept as the definition.A benchmark price feed for the CLV test.Adopt all three as separate gates. A strategy must pass calibration first; CLV and persistence are additional. Never average them into one score.S03
Q11How are correlation values computed for parlays and prop combos, and from what joint data?HighYes — for parlay and combo productsCorrelation requires repeated joint observations of the same pairing. Team-level scoring is available from official standings (S03), so team totals can supply joint observations.Whether player-level joint outcomes can be assembled at all from official sources at the depth needed, and how many observations per pair are required.Player game logs sufficient to estimate pairwise prop correlations.Start correlations at the team-total level where data exists. For player props, publish the method and mark correlations blank until the joint sample is adequate.S03
Q12What counts as out-of-sample, and how is the holdout protected from repeated peeking?HighYesOfficial data is organised by season and week, so strict time-based splits are natural: train on earlier weeks, test on later ones.Whether enough seasons are available to the project for a meaningful multi-season holdout.Multiple completed seasons of the same official fields.Walk-forward validation only, no random shuffling. Freeze the holdout in a separate file and log every access to it.S03

Publish the baseline alongside the strategy.S03

How these questions were derived

Each question was written by asking what a number on this site would require, then checking whether that requirement exists in the verified sources. Where it does not, the gap became a question. That is why the list is dominated by data-availability questions: the analytical methods are not the bottleneck, the inputs are.

FL1 — NFL Line Lab — Verified data, open questions, and a strategy leaderboard that refuses to guess.

Data captured 2026-11-14 (UTC) from official league and club sources. Every value links to its source. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. Nothing on this site is a bet, an offer to wager, or advice. No price, edge or expected-value figure is displayed unless an official source supports it.
