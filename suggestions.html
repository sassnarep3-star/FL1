Suggestions

A review surface, not a task list. Each entry states the problem, the proposal, the evidence behind it, its cost and risk, and the options available — then recommends one. Nothing here has been implemented as a change to the analysis; these are paths for someone to choose between.

How to use this page. Read the options, not just the recommendation. Every recommendation is reversible, and the reversibility of each one is stated, so a wrong choice is cheap to undo.

SG01 Publish a fair value only, never a fake market price

The problem

The brief asks to originate lines. There is no official source of market prices in this repo, so any displayed price that looks like a market quote would be invented.

Evidence

data/sources.tsv S03 — official standings supply team scoring, but no official price feed was found.

The proposal

Output one number per game: our fair-value probability, with an explicit uncertainty band and a timestamp. Display the market price column as blank until the user supplies a benchmark feed.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Fair value only, price column blank and labelled. (Recommended)
  2. Fair value plus a user-supplied price feed.
  3. Wait for a price feed before publishing anything.

Recommendation. A. A blank, labelled column is honest and costs nothing. Option C delays all work on the strength of a data source that was never promised.

Expected impact: Protects the whole site from being built on an invented number.

SG02 Grade every strategy on calibration first, profit second

The problem

Ranking strategies by win rate or units won rewards luck. Over a few dozen bets the noise band is wider than any realistic edge.

Evidence

data/questions.json Q09, Q10 — the season is 18 weeks and Week 10 is in progress (S05).

The proposal

Make calibration the primary gate: bucket predictions by stated probability and check that the realised hit rate matches. Rank on calibration error and sample size, and show profit only after the sample gate is met.

Cost and risk

Effort Medium   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Calibration-primary ranking. (Recommended)
  2. Profit-primary ranking with a minimum sample gate.
  3. Rank on both and display them side by side without combining.

Recommendation. A, with C as a display layer. Profit-first ranking will surface a lucky strategy in week one and destroy the credibility of the board.

Expected impact: Turns the leaderboard from a lottery into a measurement.

SG03 Freeze predictions before kickoff with a cryptographic seal

The problem

A strategy record is worthless if a prediction can be edited after the result is known. Without a seal, no reader can distinguish a genuine forecast from hindsight.

Evidence

data/questions.json Q02, Q12.

The proposal

Write each prediction to a timestamped file before kickoff and record a SHA-256 hash of the prediction set. Publish the hashes. After the game, publish the results; the hash proves the forecast did not change.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Hash-sealed prediction log committed before kickoff. (Recommended)
  2. Public timestamping service.
  3. No seal, trust the author.

Recommendation. A. It is nearly free, and it is the single cheapest thing that makes the leaderboard credible.

Expected impact: Converts claims into an auditable record.

SG04 Split the backup-deficiency feature into 'observed absence' and 'derived depth'

The problem

The brief wants 2nd/3rd/4th-string replacements tracked. There is no league-wide official depth chart (S13 returns 404), no official snap-count feed in this repo, and club depth charts are self-labelled 'Unofficial' (S08).

Evidence

data/sources.tsv S13 (404), S08 (club page titled 'Unofficial Depth Chart'), S07 (roster status codes), S01 (injury report).

The proposal

Show two clearly separated tables. (1) Observed absence: straight from the official injury report, no inference. (2) Derived depth: built from roster status codes plus each club's own published depth chart, and labelled derived everywhere it appears.

Cost and risk

Effort High   Risk Medium
Reversibility: Reversible — the derived layer can be removed without touching the observed layer.

Options

  1. Two-layer build, observed and derived kept strictly separate. (Recommended)
  2. Observed absence only; defer depth entirely.
  3. Build derived depth and present it as if it were official.

Recommendation. A. Option C is the trap: it would look like the most impressive feature on the site while being the least defensible.

Expected impact: Delivers the requested feature without asserting something the sources cannot support.

SG05 Add a Flagged for Review page as a first-class section

The problem

Irregularities and unverifiable values currently have nowhere to live. The alternative is quietly filling them in, which is exactly what the brief forbids.

Evidence

data/sources.tsv S12-S14 (404s), data/questions.json Q08 (cross-source contradictions).

The proposal

A permanent page listing every unverified value, every 404 source, every cross-source contradiction, and every open question, each with the source link that was attempted and the date. Nothing is hidden and nothing is guessed.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Dedicated page, always populated, never empty. (Recommended)
  2. A footnote at the bottom of the home page.
  3. Leave unreviewed items out of the site entirely.

Recommendation. A. It is the mechanism that lets the rest of the site be trusted.

Expected impact: Makes the honesty of the dataset visible instead of asserted.

SG06 Adopt walk-forward validation with a frozen, access-logged holdout

The problem

Tuning and scoring on the same weeks produces an optimistic number that will not survive contact with future games.

Evidence

data/questions.json Q12.

The proposal

Train on weeks 1..k, predict week k+1, roll forward. Never shuffle. Keep the final weeks in a separate frozen file and log every single time it is read.

Cost and risk

Effort Medium   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Strict walk-forward with a logged holdout. (Recommended)
  2. k-fold cross-validation on shuffled games.
  3. Single train/test split.

Recommendation. A. Option B leaks future information into the past in a sport with strong week-to-week drift.

Expected impact: Prevents the most common way an in-house model exaggerates its own accuracy.

SG07 Start with team totals, and defer parlays until legs are individually validated

The problem

The brief asks for single bets, parlays and prop combos at once. Parlays multiply variance, so a failed parlay does not reveal which leg was wrong, and props need player-level joint data this repo does not yet have.

Evidence

data/questions.json Q01, Q11.

The proposal

Sequence the work: team-level single market first, then player props once player data exists, then 2-leg combos only after each leg independently clears its own gate, then correlation estimation.

Cost and risk

Effort Medium   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible — it is an ordering, not a removal.

Options

  1. Sequenced build. (Recommended)
  2. All three product types in parallel.
  3. Singles only, forever.

Recommendation. A. Option B is how projects end up with a parlay board backed by three unvalidated legs.

Expected impact: Keeps every published number downstream of a validated one.

SG08 Require every strategy to beat a published naive baseline

The problem

A model that only rediscovers 'the better team usually wins' looks skilful when compared to nothing.

Evidence

data/sources.tsv S03 — official PF/PA for all 32 clubs.

The proposal

Publish a named baseline, for example a points-differential rating from official season scoring. A strategy is only ranked if it beats that baseline out of sample.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Mandatory baseline comparison. (Recommended)
  2. Baseline shown for information but not required.
  3. No baseline.

Recommendation. A. Without it the leaderboard measures effort, not edge.

Expected impact: Makes the leaderboard's ranking mean something.

SG09 Timestamp and never overwrite every injury snapshot

The problem

The injury report changes several times a week. A single undated snapshot cannot support any claim about timing, and mixing snapshots introduces look-ahead bias.

Evidence

data/sources.tsv S01; data/questions.json Q06.

The proposal

One immutable file per capture, named by UTC timestamp. All analysis reads a specific snapshot and records which one.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Immutable timestamped snapshots. (Recommended)
  2. One file overwritten each week.
  3. Capture only once, at the end of the week.

Recommendation. A. Option C discards the entire within-week signal, which is the only part that is not already in the closing price.

Expected impact: Preserves the ability to ask timing questions later.

Cost and risk

Effort Medium   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  2. Static site generator with a build action.
  3. Single-page app with a bundler.

Recommendation. A. Nothing to break, nothing to version, and Pages serves it as-is.

Expected impact: The published site cannot drift from the committed data.

Options

  2. Prize pool after review.
  3. No leaderboard at all.

Recommendation. A. It delivers the requested competitive structure with none of the exposure.

Expected impact: Keeps the project squarely informational.

SG12 Separate 'observed' from 'derived' in every table, visibly

The problem

Mixing values read directly from a source with values computed from them makes the whole table unauditable.

Evidence

All data files in this repo.

The proposal

Tag each row with its provenance: OBSERVED (read verbatim from a linked official page) or DERIVED (computed, with the formula stated). Blanks are never filled.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Visible provenance tag per table. (Recommended)
  2. Provenance documented in a separate methodology page only.
  3. No provenance marking.

Recommendation. A. A reader should never have to guess whether a number was read or computed.

Expected impact: Makes line-by-line verification possible, which is what the brief asks for.

SG13 Expand depth coverage one club at a time, starting with a single worked example

The proposal

Ship one club fully worked and verified, with the matchup relevance explained, then expand club by club behind the same validation.

Cost and risk

Effort Low per club   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  2. All 32 clubs immediately.
  3. None until all 32 are ready.

Recommendation. A. It demonstrates the method end-to-end and keeps every published cell checkable.

SG14 Reconcile contradictions by publishing them, never by choosing silently

Evidence

data/questions.json Q08; data/sources.tsv S01, S10, S15.

The proposal

Leave both values as captured, link both sources, and list the contradiction on the Flagged for Review page with a note that no resolution was applied.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Publish both, resolve nothing. (Recommended)
  2. Pick the newer source.
  3. Drop the player from the table.

Recommendation. A. Any silent choice would be an unlogged inference, which is what the brief prohibits.

Expected impact: Keeps the dataset honest and makes the source's own errors reviewable.

SG16 Choose a betting-price source before any market column or ROI is computed

  2. Owner supplies a licensed feed, labelled 'licensed, not official', with its terms recorded.

SG17 Confirm the demotion-gate thresholds (G-14) before any strategy goes live

The problem

The G-14 windows (100 bets to demote, 250 to re-promote) and the 95% lower-bound rule are proposals, not facts about football.

Evidence

engine/gates.py (demotion_decision, repromotion_decision); governance.html worked examples; tests/test_brief.py.

The proposal

The owner accepts or changes the windows and the bound. The rule shape (exposure, sample, signal, CLV) stays as written.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Low while no strategy is live
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Keep the proposed defaults (100 to demote, 250 to re-promote, 95% lower bound). (Recommended for a paper trial.)
  2. Longer windows (250 to demote) to make demotion slower and less noisy.
  3. Add a drawdown trigger, which needs an owner-supplied number.

Recommendation. A. The defaults are the smallest honest windows the code supports. A drawdown number is a policy choice and should come from the owner.

Expected impact: Sets how quickly a live strategy is returned to paper.

SG18 Re-read the official Week 1-9 pages game by game before any backtest is quoted

The problem

Only Week 10 results were re-read per game from an official page. Weeks 1-9 are reconciled to the standings in aggregate only (F22).

Evidence

data/results.tsv (137 rows); S50 (official week pages); F22.

The proposal

Read the nine official week pages and compare every game row (a line-by-line check), recording the result in data/verification-brief.tsv.

Cost and risk

Effort Medium (nine page reads)   Risk Low
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Do the nine-page line-by-line check now. (Recommended.)
  2. Accept aggregate reconciliation for now and label every backtest accordingly.
  3. Drop Weeks 1-9 from the model inputs until they are verified.

Recommendation. A. The backtest is only as good as its inputs, and the brief asks for line-by-line verification.

Expected impact: Turns the results feed from aggregate-reconciled into per-game verified.

SG19 Decide whether any third-party-derived correlation may ever be shown

The problem

Brief B6 says correlations come only from official numbers, with n shown. Every existing coefficient comes from the nflverse mirror, so all are withheld (F19).

Evidence

prop-correlations.html (withheld, n shown); measured.html (withdrawal banner); flagged F19.

The proposal

The owner confirms the strict rule (withheld) or allows third-party values under a visible non-official label.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Medium if a third-party coefficient were used as a bet input
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Keep withheld; no coefficient is published. (Recommended, and the brief's current rule.)
  2. Show third-party coefficients with n and a 'non-official' label, and never use them to size a bet.
  3. Find an official joint sample (for example official box scores) before computing any correlation.

Recommendation. A. The brief is explicit. Option C is the right long-term path and needs a source that has not been found yet.

Expected impact: Decides whether the correlation pages ever show a number.

SG20 Acquire one licensed odds feed and start open/close capture

The problem

The price-snapshot store is built and tested, but no verified price source exists, so the brief's open/close requirement cannot be filled (F18, Q21).

Evidence

price-snapshots.html; engine/price_snapshots.py; data/price_snapshots.csv (56 rows, opens blank).

The proposal

Choose one licensed feed (a commercial odds API, or a single designated book captured at fixed times), record open at market open and close at kickoff minus one minute per market, into engine/price_snapshots.py. The capture job is append-only and keyed to the model version.

Cost and risk

Effort Medium   Risk Low - the store rejects unattributed quotes, so a bad feed cannot silently pollute it.
Reversibility: Fully reversible - the store is append-only; a withdrawn feed leaves its rows attributed and quarantined.

Options

  1. Commercial odds API subscription. (Recommended if budget exists)
  2. Manual capture from one designated book at fixed times, documented per row.
  3. Stay with closes only and say so on the page.

Recommendation. A if the terms allow public display; otherwise B. Option C keeps the site honest but leaves opening-line movement unmeasurable.

Expected impact: Fills the open columns, unlocks CLV and decay monitoring (D1), and lets validation gate G6 finally run.

SG21 Pre-register the demotion and promotion thresholds in one commit before any live stake

The problem

D1-D3 are implemented with documented default thresholds; defaults chosen in advance are proposals, but defaults left unfrozen can still be edited after a losing streak.

Evidence

engine/demotion.py; validation.html; tests/test_requirements_core8.py.

The proposal

One commit, signed off, that freezes: the decay window, the calibration band, the concentration caps, the promotion gate list (G1-G6 all PASS) and the INSUFFICIENT_DATA floor. Review only on the gate-review schedule.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Reversible only by a later pre-registered amendment, never silently.

Options

  1. Freeze now with the documented defaults. (Recommended)
  2. Wait for the first season's data to choose thresholds.
  3. Leave thresholds editable per strategy.

Recommendation. A. Option B manufactures thresholds from results; option C reintroduces exactly the discretion the gates exist to remove.

Expected impact: Makes demotion automatic and non-negotiable, which is the only version of it worth having.

SG22 Extend the results feed with player-level stats from an official source once one is identified

The problem

Player-prop combos cannot be backtested from team-level results; the only player-level logs in the repo are third-party, so prop correlations stay blocked on the official-correlations page (CO-07).

Evidence

correlations-official.html (CO-07 blocked); prop-correlations.html (third-party estimates, labelled).

The proposal

Register an official player-statistics source (NFL.com gamebooks or the league's official statistics pages at season grain), capture it into an append-only feed like the results feed, and only then compute prop-pair correlations officially.

Cost and risk

Effort High   Risk Medium - official gamebooks are per-week documents; season-grain capture is real work.
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Capture official gamebooks weekly into the feed. (Recommended)
  2. Accept labelled third-party player logs for research, official-only for claims.
  3. Drop player-prop combos from scope.

Recommendation. A with B as the interim. C abandons part of the brief.

Expected impact: Unblocks prop backtests, player-prop combos and CO-07 on official footing.

SG23 Schedule a weekly append-only capture of the injury report and depth charts

The problem

Everything on this site is one snapshot from 2026-11-14. A single snapshot cannot show practice-trajectory effects, week-over-week availability deltas (SIG-04), or any persistence claim.

Evidence

EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md (one-snapshot blocker); data/injury_snapshot_b.json (two renders differ).

The proposal

A scheduled job (the repo already has a weekly refresh workflow) that appends the official injury report and each club's depth chart as dated, immutable snapshots, reconciling renders rather than overwriting (the report's row count varies between renders).

Cost and risk

Effort Medium   Risk Low - append-only; a bad capture is a new dated row, not a destructive edit.
Reversibility: Fully reversible

Options

  1. Weekly scheduled append. (Recommended)
  2. Manual capture before each slate.
  3. Keep single snapshot and scope claims to Week 10.

Recommendation. A. It is the cheapest way to turn a poster into a dataset.

Expected impact: Unlocks within-season deltas, availability trend signals, and multi-week validation.

SG24 Decide the fair-line publication policy before the first fitted line ships

The problem

The board publishes fair, market and difference as separate columns. Once a model exists, a policy is needed for whether a fair line publishes with an uncertainty band always, only after acceptance (Q22), or only alongside the naive baseline.

Evidence

line-board.html; line-origination.html (uncertainty section).

The proposal

Fix the policy now: every published fair line carries its uncertainty band and the naive baseline beside it; unaccepted model output never reaches the board; sealed before kickoff.

Cost and risk

Effort Low   Risk Very low
Reversibility: Fully reversible until the first line ships; awkward afterwards.

Options

  1. Fair line always with band + baseline. (Recommended)
  2. Fair line only after acceptance, without band.
  3. Publish raw model output with a caveat.

Recommendation. A. Option C is how invented numbers enter a site that promised never to publish them.

Expected impact: Keeps the board's blank-cell promise intact once the model exists.

FL1 — NFL Line Lab — Verified data, open questions, and a strategy leaderboard that refuses to guess.

Data captured 2026-11-14 (UTC) from official league and club sources. Every value links to its source. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review. Nothing on this site is a bet, an offer to wager, or advice. No price, edge or expected-value figure is displayed unless an official source supports it.
