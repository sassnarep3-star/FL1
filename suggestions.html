Open Questions & Required Information

Q-01

Which official data source will power injury and roster tracking?

Injury status, practice participation, and starter/backup depth must come from a source that can be verified and re-checked.

Answer: NFL.com official injury report (nfl.com/injuries/).

Missing-info discovery: Confirm nfl.com exposes a machine-readable feed; if not, document manual scrape cadence and flag.

Q-02

Which source will provide NFL betting lines, parlays, and player props?

There is no official NFL source for betting lines. A third-party odds provider must be selected before any line-origination strategy can be validated.

Answer: blank — no official source identified yet.

Missing-info discovery: Research licensed odds APIs (e.g., official sportsbook data partners), record candidate providers, compare cost/latency/coverage, flag as unverified until selected.

Q-03

What validation gates must each betting strategy pass?

Strategies must be shown to be selectively skill-based, not luck-based, before inclusion on the leaderboard.

Answer: blank — gates not yet defined.

Missing-info discovery: Propose gates: minimum sample size, edge vs. closing line, win-rate threshold, correlation limit, max drawdown, out-of-sample test. Reviewer chooses final gate set.

Q-04

How are correlation values determined between bet legs (parlays / player props)?

Correlation must be measured so correlated legs are not double-counted as independent edge.

Answer: blank — correlation method not defined.

Missing-info discovery: Propose Pearson/Spearman correlation on historical outcomes, conditional probability P(B|A), and variance-inflation checks. Reviewer selects method.

Q-05

How is the leaderboard scored and how are rewards allocated?

Top-performing strategies must be rewarded based on verified, skill-based performance.

Answer: blank — scoring/reward rules not defined.

Missing-info discovery: Propose scoring by risk-adjusted return (e.g., Sharpe-like), hit rate, and edge vs. close; rewards proportional to rank. Reviewer approves.

Q-06

Must identify when 2nd, 3rd, or 4th string backups replace starters on either side of the ball.

Answer: blank — deficiency thresholds not defined.

Missing-info discovery: Track snap counts and AV (approx value) drop-off starter-to-backup; flag games where a key starter is out and the backup has limited NFL snaps.
