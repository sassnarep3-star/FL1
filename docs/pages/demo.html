They agree on all 22 players both of them rendered (0 disagreements), yet the second contains 46 players the first never showed, including 4 confirmed 'Out' designations. That is the specific failure this reconciliation exists to catch: a truncated render is not wrong, it is quietly short, and it looks like a quiet week. Ingestion therefore fetches until row counts stabilise, records (timestamp, row count, chunk coverage) per capture, reconciles at least two renders, and never overwrites.
