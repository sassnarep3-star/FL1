Correlation, parlays and prop combos

The brief asks us to determine correlation values. We can honestly deliver the estimator, the treatment rules and the empty cells — not the numbers, because a correlation coefficient in this project must be measured from a joint sample we hold, and the sample (historical prices and settled joint outcomes) is exactly what is blocked at GAP-01. Publishing invented rho values would be the single most damaging fabrication available here: every parlay decision downstream would inherit it. So the rho column is blank by design and flagged.

A. What we are measuring

Not 'are these events related' — they obviously are — but 'how much does treating them as independent overstate the combined price'.

rho (pair)Pearson correlation of the two leg win indicators, on the population of games both legs were offered on, at the moment of pricing (pre-game).This is the quantity a parlay price actually depends on, not the correlation of underlying stat lines. Computed as corr(I_A, I_B) over our settled sample; reported with a bootstrap interval and an n.
Odds product errorfair_parlay_prob / (p_A x p_B) - 1, i.e. how wrong multiplying single-leg probabilities is.For positively correlated legs this is positive: the true combined probability is higher than the product, so a bettor multiplying singles believes they have more value than they do. For negatively correlated legs it inverts. This ratio, not rho, is what a parlay decision should read.
Joint-vs-marginal modelSimulate the game (score process, drive outcomes, player stat generation), evaluate both legs inside one simulation, and read the joint probability directly.Preferred to analytic correlation because props are non-linear functions of the same simulated game (a QB's yards and his receiver's catches share drives, possessions and game script, not a linear factor). The simulator is engine/correlation.py; its inputs are our own distributions, and it inherits S2's unverified parameters honestly.
Break-even (singles)1 / (1 + decimal payout); at -110 that is 52.38%.Derived, and cross-checked against the note in the fetched academic source; used as the hurdle in G-2.
Parlay hurdleProduct of the individual legs' break-evens, corrected by the measured dependence.The reason parlays are dangerous even when each leg has an edge: if the operator's combined price already reflects the dependence, our apparent 'extra value from correlation' is not extra.
House-rule constraintA non-SGP parlay may never contain two offers whose outcomes might be related; if they slip through, the operator may void the affected legs; an SGP with a void or pushed leg is repriced from the odds available at bet placement.Verified from operator house rules as filed with a state regulator, and mirrored on a licensed property's own rules document. Regulator footnoteNevada requires books to maintain win-to-write percentages for each sport and for sports parlay cards as a separate line.The regulatory framework itself treats parlays as a structurally different product. What the actual percentage is, we have not verified, so no number is published (GAP-08).

C. Reading the blank rho column

Why the most valuable thing this table does is refuse to fill itself in.

C. Reading the blank rho column
ItemExplanation
The blank is the findingWe were asked to determine correlation values. The determination is that the values require a joint sample of prices and settled outcomes we do not yet hold, and that the estimate cannot be sourced from any official NFL or regulator page — the league does not publish betting lines at all. A number written here today would be decoration.
What we can compute without any purchaseCorrelations between two markets whose outcomes are determined by official game results (spreads, totals, team totals, stat props) can be measured from historical scores alone, and score history is derivable from official game data we are permitted to collect for internal use. That is the first thing to build after the collection decision (Q-09) is made: it closes C-01, C-02, C-04, C-10 and most of the prop pairs at zero cost. It gives outcome correlation, but not correlation-at-the-priced-moment, which is what parlay value actually needs.
What needs moneyThe pricing side: the operator's posted SGP price against its own singles at the same second. That is the only way to test whether the operator has already taken the correlation, and it is the difference between an edge and an artefact. Hence S-01.
Practical rule until thenSingles only. A parlay built on an assumed correlation is a bet whose variance we cannot bound, and a strategy whose variance we cannot bound cannot be sized, which means it fails G-1 and G-10 by construction regardless of its hit rate.
