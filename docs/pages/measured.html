7. Verdict

ALL PASS — spread-derived numbers may be published as internal measurements — settled: `spread_line` is the away team's line (verified against moneyline direction in data/verification-d.tsv V8, 13/13), which is reading B — the reading whose plausibility checks pass. Reading A, which the column's own documentation implies, produces a 60% home-cover rate and a 36% home-favourite share, and is published here only as the audit trail of how the question was decided. Spread-derived numbers in this module are therefore computed under B; the remaining caution is provenance, not sign.

Verification rule. Every factual row links to an official source — the league (nfl.com and its subdomains), a state gaming regulator, or a peer-reviewed publication. A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is left blank and flagged rather than estimated. Source verification levels: FETCHED = page opened and read · INDEX-MEANS = seen through a search index of the official document only · LINKED = reached from an official page, not opened · UNREACHABLE = tried and failed. See the source register and the flagged-for-review page.
