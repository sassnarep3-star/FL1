The one thing to understand before reading any table. The brief asks us to originate lines and rank strategies by skill; the repo contains no betting-line data, and none is available free from an official source — the league's betting data feed is distributed exclusively. So this release ships the apparatus: sourced registers, estimators, gates, a compliance veto and a gap map. The values that would make a strategy claim real are blank and flagged, and the register says precisely what to acquire to fill them. The first purchase decision (a licensed line history) unblocks more than any engineering work available today.

Verification rule. Every factual row links to an official source — the league (nfl.com and its subdomains), a state gaming regulator, or a peer-reviewed publication. A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is left blank and flagged rather than estimated. Source verification levels: FETCHED = page opened and read · INDEX-MEANS = seen through a search index of the official document only · LINKED = reached from an official page, not opened · UNREACHABLE = tried and failed.
