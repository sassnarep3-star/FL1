Line origination: model specification

A specification, not a validated model. Every free parameter is left blank with the estimator that would fill it; a coefficient appearing here as a number would be a fabrication, so none do. The architecture is deliberately totals-first, availability-adjusted and market-neutral at the base, so the market enters only as a benchmark at the end (that is what makes 'did we beat the close' answerable).

A. Pipeline stages

Each stage is independently testable and independently releasable; a stage's output is a distribution, never a point, because a point hides the uncertainty that the gates measure.

B. Signal inventory (candidates, each with a falsifiable test)

A signal is a hypothesis with a stated direction, the market it applies to, the gate that kills it, and the reason it might be real. Nothing here is claimed to work. Publishing them before testing is the point: it makes the graveyard auditable and the multiple-comparison count honest.

C. What we refuse to state

The line between a specification and a fabrication, drawn explicitly so a reviewer can check we kept it.

C. What we refuse to state
KeyItem
RefusedAny coefficient, prior mean, or effect size presented as settled. Every parameter above is blank with an estimator attached, because inventing a number to make the model look complete is the failure mode the brief prohibits.
RefusedAny percentage mapping from a designation to a probability of playing. The league's definitions are qualitative, and the widely-quoted 'Questionable = 50%' has no official basis we could verify.
RefusedAny Nevada or per-market hold percentage. The regulator documents exist and are the right source; we have not opened them at page level, so the figure is a gap (GAP-08), not a number.
RefusedAny historical injury row we did not fetch. Only the Week 10 2026 chunk we captured is published, and it is labelled partial.
RefusedAny claim that a strategy is profitable, or that a backtest is evidence. Until a strategy passes the gates on out-of-sample data with a measured hold, the correct statement about it is 'untested'.
RefusedAny use of non-public information about availability, and any bet whose subject is an injury, an official, a single play, or a pre-determined roster decision — the league's own prohibited classes.

Verification rule. Every factual row links to an official source — the league (nfl.com and its subdomains), a state gaming regulator, or a peer-reviewed publication. A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is left blank and flagged rather than estimated. Source verification levels: FETCHED = page opened and read · INDEX-MEANS = seen through a search index of the official document only · LINKED = reached from an official page, not opened · UNREACHABLE = tried and failed.
