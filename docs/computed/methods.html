Methods

The exact rules this build followed, so any number can be attacked on its merits.

Principles

  1. No invented numbers. If a value cannot be computed from a pinned source or read off an official page, the cell is blank and the item goes on Flagged for review.
  2. No fitting to profit. Elo constants and the Elo→spread conversion were fixed before any backtest ran. They are in the script, in scripts/strategy_lab.py, in plain sight.
  3. Walk-forward only. A signal may only use information available before the kickoff it prices. Ratings are read pre-game; form is accumulated post-game.
  4. Every strategy faces the same gates. Thresholds are global constants, not tuned per strategy.
  5. Publish the failures. The search count is public because the Bonferroni correction depends on it.

Economics

  • Bets are settled at the closing price from games.csv (spread odds, total odds, moneyline).
  • Pushes are excluded from both numerator and denominator.
  • ROI = total profit ÷ total staked, with 1 unit staked per bet.
  • The breakeven hit rate is computed exactly from the realised average decimal price, not assumed to be 52.38%.
  • Significance is a one-sided t-test on per-bet return, with a Wilson 95% interval on hit rate.

Gates

  • G1 ≥150 settled bets and ≥60 out-of-sample settled bets.
  • G2 ROI > 0 in-sample and out-of-sample.
  • G3 one-sided p < 0.05 on per-bet return.
  • G4 Wilson 95% lower bound on hit rate > the vig breakeven.
  • G5 no single season supplies more than 40% of total profit.
  • G6 p < 0.05/N where N is the number of variants actually searched. N is read from the signal registry at runtime, so the denominator can never drift from the real search count. With the current build N = 16, giving p < 0.00313.
  • G7 maximum drawdown smaller than total profit.

Known limitations

  • Closing prices only — no opening-line series for the full sample (FLAG-08). A real edge measured against the close is understated, and line movement cannot be studied at all.
  • Result-history Elo is information the market already has. That is why the market-divergence family fails; it is not evidence that no edge exists anywhere.
  • Injury weights are an assumption. The NFL publishes definitions, not probabilities.
  • Depth order is absent from every reachable source, so the 2nd/3rd/4th-string model stops at the quarterback.
  • 2026 data is one completed Week 10 game. Nothing here is a 2026 out-of-sample claim.
  • No prop market data, so prop-combo correlation is unmeasured.
