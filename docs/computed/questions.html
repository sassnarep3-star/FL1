What must be answered before a decision is reasonable — organised by what we have and what we lack.

These are the questions that have to be answered before a decision is reasonable. Each one states what we already have, what we are missing, why it blocks a decision, and the exact form the answer needs to take. A question with an empty "we have" column is a question we cannot currently reason about at all.

6 of 16 are critical and each one blocks a requirement. Answering them is the highest-value work available — more valuable than another backtest, because a backtest run against an unknown threshold produces a number nobody can act on.

The five that matter most

  • OQ-01 — what price can you actually get? Every ROI here is measured against the close. Without an opening-line feed or a book-specific price sample, the real edge is unknown and roughly half of any model-vs-close gap is fictional.
  • OQ-07 — where is a timestamped depth chart? Without it the 2nd/3rd/4th-string requirement is unanswerable, full stop.
  • OQ-10 — what number counts as "luck removed"? The gates are numbers. You have to pick the bar, or the standard is whatever the agent felt like that day.
  • OQ-05 — can the release CDN be reached? This one question unblocks injuries, depth charts and snap counts at once.
  • OQ-14 — who signs off on a live test? Rewards require accountability; the leaderboard currently has no one to release a strategy to.
