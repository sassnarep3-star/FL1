The board

Fourteen games. Spread is from the home team's perspective, so a negative number means the home team is favoured. Availability edge is home disadvantage minus away disadvantage — a positive number means the away team is healthier. It is not a point value and it is not a prediction; it is the measurable input.

The three games the availability signal separates most

  • HOU at CLE — edge −4.25, the largest on the board, and it runs against the home team: Houston lists five Out against Cleveland's two. Cleveland is favoured by 4.5.
  • SF at DAL — edge +4.00, Dallas lists five Out against San Francisco's one, yet San Francisco is only −4.5 on the road.
  • MIA at IND — edge +3.75, Indianapolis lists six Out against Miami's two.
Do not read these as edges. The market has already seen these reports — they are published on NFL.com before kickoff, and the closing line is set after them. An availability signal only has value if it is (a) faster than the line, or (b) weighted by starter quality, which needs the depth feed. The board shows the input; it does not claim the output.
