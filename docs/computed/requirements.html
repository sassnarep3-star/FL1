Reading the statuses

  • DONE — delivered and evidenced in the named file.
  • PARTIAL — delivered in part; the missing part is named and traced to an open question or a flagged item.
  • BLOCKED — cannot be delivered from any source reachable in this environment. The gap is documented rather than filled with an estimate.
  • REJECTED-ON-EVIDENCE — the work was done and the answer is no. That is a result, not an omission.
