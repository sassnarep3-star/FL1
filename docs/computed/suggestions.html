Site and decision-making improvements

  • Keep the rejected list public. A leaderboard that shows only winners is a leaderboard you cannot audit; the deflation gate only means something if the search count is visible.
  • Put the gate config under version control and sign it. The current thresholds are the agent's defaults, not the owner's decision (OQ-03).
  • Label every ROI with the price it is measured against. "ROI" without "vs close" is the most common way a betting backtest misleads its reader.
  • Fail the build on an unsourced row. scripts/verify_site.py already does this for table rows; extend it to HTTP-status checks on every source.
  • Show the season ledger next to every headline ROI. One good season hides inside a good average, which is exactly what gate G5 exists to catch.
  • Keep the Week 10 board as inputs, not picks. Publishing picks before the gates are satisfied would be the one thing that turns a research repo into a tout sheet.
