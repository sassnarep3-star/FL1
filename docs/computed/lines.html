Line origination

The exact model, its constants, its calibration, and why it is not yet an independent line.

How a number is originated here

The line engine is deliberately boring and fully auditable. Nothing is fitted to betting profit, because fitting to profit is how a backtest is manufactured.

  • Ratings. A causal Elo is updated after every game with a margin-of-victory multiplier and a 48-point home-field term. Between seasons every team is regressed 25% toward 1505. Ratings are read before the game they price, so there is no leakage.
  • Conversion. Elo difference becomes a win probability, and a win probability becomes a spread with a fixed constant of 2.7 points per 100 Elo (spread = 10.8 × log10(p/(1−p))). The constant was fixed before any backtest ran.
  • Comparison. That model spread is differenced against the closing spread. The difference is the only signal the market-divergence family is allowed to see.
  • Calibration check. Mean signed error −0.77 points, mean absolute error 2.89 points, correlation with the close 0.828, over 5,336 games. The model is a slightly worse version of the market, not an independent one — which is exactly why the divergence strategies lose.
Honest limitation. This is not yet “your own line” in the sense that matters. Its disagreement with the close has a standard deviation of 3.56 points, and that whole distribution is inside the range where the market is simply more accurate. Publishing a number is easy; publishing a number that is better requires information the market does not have, and result-history Elo is information the market already has. See OQ-01 and SG-08.

What an independent line would need

  • Pace and efficiency inputs the close does not fully absorb: play-by-play EPA by situation, success rate on early downs, pressure rate.
  • Availability priced per starter lost at a position, not per player — which needs the depth feed (FLAG-02).
  • An opening-line feed, so a model can be tested against a price that is actually obtainable (FLAG-08).
  • A weather forecast pulled at kickoff-minus-72h, not the measured post-game wind. The one weather candidate that looked interesting (WIND15_UNDER) uses post-game wind, which is not available before the game. That alone disqualifies it from live use even if it had passed the statistical gates.
