Why the two closest candidates were rejected

WIND15_UNDER — 425 bets, 55.1% hit against a 51.2% breakeven, +7.2% ROI, +5.7% OOS, p = 0.063. Passes G1, G2 and G7. Fails G3 (needs p < 0.05), G4 (lower bound on hit rate still crosses the breakeven at this sample size) and G6 (needs p < 0.00313). It also uses post-game measured wind, which is not knowable before kickoff, so it is not implementable as written before it is even a statistics question.

DIV_DOG_AND_ELO — 936 bets, 54.3% hit, +5.7% ROI, +8.1% OOS, p = 0.036. Passes G1, G2, G3 and G7. Fails G4 (its 95% lower bound on hit rate, ~51.1%, does not clear the 51.4% vig breakeven), G5 (one season supplies too much of the profit) and G6 (p = 0.036 is not < 0.00313). This is the classic shape of a finding that is real in-sample and survives a split, but is selected out of fifteen tries. It is the one worth re-testing on new data, not the one worth betting.

Families tested

  • market_devig — Elo vs close, win-probability gap vs the implied probability.
  • situational — rest advantage, divisional underdogs, big-favourite fade, dome totals.
  • weather — wind, cold-and-wind.
  • conjunction — rest AND Elo, divisional dog AND Elo.
  • availability — weighted injury disadvantage (2025 only; fails G1 by design).
  • control — bet every home team. Not a strategy; it measures the vig.
