Flagged for review

Nine irregularities and nine values left deliberately blank, each with impact and the fix required.

Everything that could not be verified, could not be obtained, or is a known weakness in the data or the method. Flagged items do not block publication — they block trust in the specific thing they describe, so each one names its own blast radius.

1 critical, 2 high, 4 medium, 2 low. The single highest-leverage fix is FLAG-01; four other items are consequences of it.

What to check first

  1. FLAG-02 — the 2nd/3rd/4th-string requirement. If you have a licensed depth-chart feed, this closes and the availability model becomes usable.
  2. FLAG-01 — the CDN. One allowlist entry adds injuries 2026, depth charts and snap counts, which unblocks four other items.
  3. FLAG-06 — the 2026 season is one completed game into Week 10. Nothing on this site claims a 2026 out-of-sample result, and it should stay that way until Week 18.
  4. FLAG-08 — closing-only prices. If you have an opening-line feed, the entire line-origination question changes, because a real edge can then be measured against a price you can actually get.
