## Release (dateline May 14, 2026)

"May 14, 2026" (dateline)

"NEW YORK — May 14, 2026 — The NFL announced its 18-week, 272-game regular-season schedule for 2026, which kicks off on Wednesday night, September 9, in Seattle and concludes with 16 division games in Week 18 – three on Saturday, January 9, and 13 on Sunday, January 10."

"The 2026 NFL schedule, powered by Amazon Web Services (AWS), will feature each team playing 17 regular-season games and three preseason games for the sixth consecutive year."

"The NFL’s 32 teams will each play 17 games over 18 weeks. Byes will begin in Week 5 and end in Week 14."

"The NFC will be the home conference for the 17th game in 2026."

"On Thursday, September 10 (8:35 p.m. ET, Netflix), the Los Angeles Rams and San Francisco 49ers will meet at Melbourne Cricket Ground, in partnership with the Victorian State Government and Visit Victoria, in the first-ever regular-season NFL game to be played in Australia."

"The NFL schedule features nine international games, the most-ever in a season, with five of the games exclusively televised on NFL Network."

"In Week 9, the NFL travels to Madrid, Spain, for the second-consecutive season in a matchup between Atlanta and Cincinnati (Sunday, 9:30 a.m. ET, NFL Network) at the Bernabéu Stadium, home to Real Madrid C.F."

"The international schedule will conclude in Week 11 with the first game in Mexico City since 2022 when San Francisco take[s on Minnesota] (Sunday night, 8:20 p.m. ET, NBC) at Estadio Banorte." (end of excerpt; text continues on the source page)

"The regular season will conclude with Week 18 on Saturday, January 9, and Sunday, January 10."

"Wild Card Weekend powered by Verizon for the 2026 season will feature six games, starting on Saturday, January 16."

"The winners meet two weeks later on Sunday, February 14, in Super Bowl LXI at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, California (ESPN), marking the ninth time the league’s final game will be played in Los Angeles."
