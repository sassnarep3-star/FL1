Strategy Leaderboard

A competition with gates. A strategy is listed, then it must earn a rank by clearing every gate in Validation. Until then it has no score, and the board stays empty.

0 of 6 entries carry a result in this register. This register holds the plan-level strategies over official data only. Its original claim — “No backtest has been executed and no performance figure is claimed anywhere in this repository” — was true of this register and false of the repository as a whole (F24): the computed research layer holds 16 backtests over third-party data, reported and re-ranked on signal below, and none of those has passed its gates either. Ranking rule, fixed in advance

The ranking formula is published now, before any result exists, so it cannot be chosen to suit an outcome:

  1. An entry is unranked until it passes gates G1 to G6. Blocked or partially-gated entries are shown as unranked and are excluded from the ordered table.
  2. Among ranked entries, order by calibration error ascending — the mean absolute gap between stated probability and realised frequency, across probability buckets.
  3. Break ties by larger validated sample, then by lower model complexity.
  4. Ties are never broken by profit. Profit is reported, never ranked on, because over an 18-week season it is dominated by variance.

Rewards are position and a badge. There is no money, no entry fee, no wagering and no odds on this site. The reason is set out as open question Q14 and suggestion SG11 rather than answered here.

Signal and staking are reported separately (requirement R25)

A lucky staking run must not be able to mask a bad signal. So the leaderboard has two blocks and the rank is computed from signal only: flat one-unit stake on every settled bet, ranked on the 95% lower bound of the hit rate against the break-even hurdle. The staking block — ROI at the stakes actually used, profit, drawdown — is reported beside it and can never change a rank, a badge or a reward. The arithmetic lives in engine/backtest.py (split_signal_staking) and is exercised by the test suite.

Read the hurdle column correctly. The table below re-ranks the computed research layer's 16 backtested strategies (third-party data — flagged). That dataset's own breakeven_hit column is circular — computed as 1/(1+realized ROI) — and is NOT used for ranking; it is transcribed in the data file for audit only. See F30 (severity High) and suggestion SG28. The reference hurdle here is the standard −110 two-way break-even, 0.52381. None of the 16 entries is signal-proven; all 16 are REJECTED in their own dataset's gates.

Demotion: live is a revocable state

A strategy that goes live and decays is sent back to paper by the demotion gate G-14 — signal decay, drawdown, or closing-line rot. Live is a state you have to keep earning.
