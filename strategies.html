Strategy Leaderboard

A competition with gates. A strategy is listed, then it must earn a rank by clearing every gate in Validation. Until then it has no score, and the board stays empty.

0 of 6 entries carry a result. No backtest has been executed and no performance figure is claimed anywhere in this repository. The leaderboard will stay empty until a strategy clears the gates with a price source supplied. That is the design, not a delay.

Ranking rule, fixed in advance

The ranking formula is published now, before any result exists, so it cannot be chosen to suit an outcome:

  1. An entry is unranked until it passes gates G1 to G6. Blocked or partially-gated entries are shown as unranked and are excluded from the ordered table.
  2. Among ranked entries, order by calibration error ascending — the mean absolute gap between stated probability and realised frequency, across probability buckets.
  3. Break ties by larger validated sample, then by lower model complexity.
  4. Ties are never broken by profit. Profit is reported, never ranked on, because over an 18-week season it is dominated by variance.

Rewards are position and a badge. There is no money, no entry fee, no wagering and no odds on this site. The reason is set out as open question Q14 and suggestion SG11 rather than answered here.
