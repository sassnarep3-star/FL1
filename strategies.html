NFL Lines Origination — Strategies

Research-backed approaches for originating NFL lines across single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos. Every strategy must pass all validation gates before use. Correlation values determine which multi-leg combinations are acceptable. All strategy content is analytical — it is not official NFL content and is flagged unverified against official sources.

How a strategy is validated

No bet is placed unless a strategy passes all of its validation gates. Gates are designed to remove luck and keep play selectively skill-based:

  • Edge gate: estimated edge must exceed vig (target > 2% after vig for singles; higher for multi-leg).
  • Sample gate: minimum historical setups (n) before a strategy is live.
  • Injury/depth gate: no key starter Out at the relevant position (cross-checked against the Injuries page).
  • Correlation gate: multi-leg combos must stay within the correlation cap for that bet type.
  • Out-of-sample gate: strategy must perform on data not used to build it.
