Line Origination & Price Store

Our own fair lines for the 13 pending Week 10 games, the order they are built in, and the price store that would hold opening and closing numbers. Model output only. Nothing here is a bet or advice.

Origination order

  1. Side first. Team ratings are fitted by least squares to the 137 reconciled 2026 results (home margin on team ratings plus a home-field term; neutral venues get no home-field term). The fair home line is the negative of the predicted home margin, rounded to the nearest half point.
  2. Total second. Expected points for each side use season-to-date points for and against: (home PF/g + away PA/g) / 2 for the home side, and the mirror for the away side. The step does not read the side, but it runs after it by design.
  3. Moneyline third. The win probability is Φ(predicted home margin / σ), where σ = %.2f is the out-of-sample RMSE of the side model. The price is the fair American price for that probability, with no vig, so it is a fair price and not a market price.

Fair, market, difference. These are three separate columns for each market. The fair value comes from this model. The market value would come from an official price source, and none exists, so every market cell is blank (F18). The difference is computed only when both sides exist, so it is blank too. The backtest does not show that the model beats a constant benchmark on margin or total (F20), so no fair line on this page is a recommendation.
