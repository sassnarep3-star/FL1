Official Correlations

Correlation values computed only from officially sourced numbers, every one published with its n. The pair columns come from the official injury report (S01), the official standings (S02/S03) and the results reconciled against them — nothing else. Estimates built on third-party prices or player logs are deliberately not promoted here (open question Q23); where they exist they live on Prop Correlations and in docs/computed/, labelled as third-party.

Intervals are paired bootstrap 95 percent (2000 resamples, seed 20261114), computed by tools/build_correlations_official.py. Spearman uses mid-ranks so tied ordinals (the injury report's three-level scales) do not distort the coefficient. A blocked row keeps its n (the number of usable joint observations, 0 here) and its reason — it is not deleted and not estimated.

Why some rows are blank

Two of the three blocked rows are blocked by the official-only rule itself: the same-game leg correlation (cover vs over) needs spread and total prices, and player-prop pairs need player-level game logs. No official source publishes either. The third (week-10 availability vs outcome) is blocked by time — the week has not settled — and becomes computable when it does. Publishing any of them from third-party inputs would break the rule this page exists to enforce.
