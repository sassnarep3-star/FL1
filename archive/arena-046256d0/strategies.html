Lines-Origination Strategy Framework

Proposed methodology for the leaderboard competition. Originate NFL lines and generate useful signals that are selectively skill-based; remove luck from performance evaluation so rewards go to genuine edge. Proposed — Pending Review

Strategy classes

Single bet

One wager per signal (spread, total, moneyline). Signal sources: line movement vs. model, injury-driven availability gaps, situational factors (rest, travel, schedule density), and single-market inefficiency. Each bet must state the signal, model estimate, and market price.

Parlay

Multiple legs in one ticket. Legs are combined only after the correlation check (below). Legs must keep a positive edge after correlation adjustment; correlated legs are rejected by the gates.

Player prop combos

Player props (passing/rushing/receiving yards, TDs) as singles or parlays. Requires position-specific modeling (snap share, usage, opponent deficiency). Prop lines must be verified from an official sportsbook before use — currently unverified (UNV-2).

Signal inputs (verified weekly)

  • Official injury report → team availability gaps NFL.com
  • Standings / efficiency (PF–PA) NFL.com
  • Schedule factors (rest, travel, international games) NFL.com

Validation gates — every strategy must pass all

GateRule (proposed)Purpose
G1 Sample sizeMinimum independent bets/events before eligibility (proposed: 50+ bets or 1 season of data).Avoid small-sample noise.
G2 Edge thresholdModel-implied edge over the market price must exceed a minimum (proposed: 2% expected value).Filter out no-edge signals.
G3 Statistical significanceEdge must be significant at a set confidence level (z-score / binomial test on realized vs. expected).Remove luck.
G4 Correlation checkBet components within a parlay must be below the correlation limit (proposed: |r| < 0.6 between legs).Avoid correlated-combo distortion.
G5 Closing-line valueStrategy must show non-negative closing-line value over the evaluation window.Confirms the edge is real vs. the market.
G6 Regime checkRe-test across regimes (offense vs. defense weeks; home/road splits).Confirms the signal is not regime-bound.
G7 Discipline checkNo look-ahead, no selective exclusion, fixed sizing rules.Prevents curve-fitting and survivorship bias.

A strategy failing any gate stays "in validation" and does not appear on the leaderboard.

Determining correlation values

  • Compute pairwise correlation (Pearson; Spearman as a robustness check) between: bet components (parlay legs), strategy returns across market factors (totals vs. spreads), and signal inputs (injury score, team efficiency, situational factors).
  • Use a rolling window (proposed: last 3 seasons where data permits) to keep correlations current.
  • Reject or re-weight any parlay whose legs exceed the correlation limit (G4); publish the correlation matrix per strategy for review.

Removing luck from the equation

  • Evaluate on expected value and closing-line value — not short-term ROI.
  • Require the minimum bet count (G1) before any strategy is ranked.
  • Use significance testing (G3): a hot streak alone cannot qualify a strategy.
  • Report variance bands alongside ROI; rank by ROI with a z-score tie-breaker.
  • Track in-sample vs. out-of-sample performance to penalize overfitting.

Leaderboard competition & rewards

ElementProposed design
EligibilityAll strategies that pass gates G1–G7.
Ranking metricVerified ROI over the evaluation window.
Tie-breakerZ-score of edge (higher is better).
Reward tiersTier 1 (top strategy) — primary reward; Tier 2 — secondary reward; Tier 3 (qualified) — recognition only.
ReleaseRewards released only after the evaluation window closes and gates are re-verified.
Re-entryStrategies re-enter each cycle; a strategy that fails a gate is removed until it re-passes.

Data dependencies (must close before live operation)

  • Verified live lines (UNV-1) and player props (UNV-2) — see the Questions page.
  • Historical closing lines and results (Q10) for backtesting and closing-line value.
  • Bankroll/unit policy (Q8) for sizing and reward normalization.
  • Injury data continuity — the official NFL.com injury report is the weekly baseline (already integrated).

Execution guardrails

  • Fixed unit sizing from the approved bankroll policy.
  • No manual overrides of gate results; overrides are logged and reviewed.
  • Weekly refresh: re-run gates, update the leaderboard, re-publish tables with source links.
