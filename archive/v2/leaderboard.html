Strategy Leaderboard Competition

Treat strategy development as a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. Strategies are ranked by verified performance, not by volume or luck.

Competition design

  • Eligibility: a strategy must pass all validation gates (see Strategies page) before entering the leaderboard.
  • Scoring metrics: ROI (return on investment), win rate, edge realized, number of bets, and a Sharpe-like risk-adjusted score (return per unit of variance).
  • Ranking: primary sort by risk-adjusted score; tie-break by total profit units.
  • Rewards: top 3 strategies each period receive allocation increase / capital reward and are flagged for live review before any real deployment.
  • Removal: any strategy that falls below its edge gate or breaches a correlation cap is removed from the leaderboard.
  • No manual input: leaderboard entries are populated from tracked strategy results, not entered by hand.
